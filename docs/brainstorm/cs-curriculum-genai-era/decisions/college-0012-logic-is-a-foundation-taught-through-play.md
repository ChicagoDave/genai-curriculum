# college-0012 — Logic is a foundation, taught through play

**Status**: ACCEPTED
**Date**: 2026-09-27

## Context

Decision college-0001 gave every roster candidate the same dive and a membership verdict of expectation, discipline, or skill. Eighteen candidates fit. Logic did not: no acceptor states it, it is not a way of meeting an expectation, and it is not a skill exercised across expectations but the faculty every skill runs on. Q-4 (2026-09-25) had already placed it as a foundation in year one, the CS-facing form of the math requirement, without saying how it is taught. The logic dive (2026-09-27) asked whether the year-one course is discrete math reframed or something built backwards from the artifacts year two needs. The answer was neither: it is play.

## Decision

- **The membership verdict gains a fourth category, foundation, for logic alone.** Decision college-0001's template is amended by pointer. A foundation is what the other three categories run on; a second one would need its own case.
- **Logic is taught in year one through play**: board games, text games, and video games; logic puzzles; geometry proofs; innovation exercises. Chosen because each pushes back, and the faculty is built by exercising it on things that do. It is intended to be the most fun part of the degree.
- **Two halves, both in year one.** A rigorous half (puzzles, proofs, the rules of games) builds the faculty that judges; a generative half (innovation exercises, designing games) builds the faculty that proposes. Design judgment needs both, and neither is deferred.
- **The year's artifacts are the play's own**: a game's rules written as constraints, a puzzle's reasoning, a proof defended against a counter-case, a text game narrated as intent versus result, alternatives ranked with reasons, a contradiction found. Graded on the reasoning, not the answer.
- **After year one, logic is exercised, never expanded.** It has no unit after the foundation year; it lives in every behavior statement, invariant, test, matrix, and reading.

## Consequences

- Decision college-0001 is amended by pointer: the verdict categories are expectation, discipline, skill, and, for logic alone, foundation.
- Q-4 stands and gains its how. The roster closes at 19 of 19 with this record.
- Open: who accepts the foundation year as the math requirement, the math department or this faculty; whether a game designed as rules and played by the cohort is the year-one capstone and the first slice without software; which games, per cohort.
- The text-game narration (intent, result, divergence) is the same sentence the year-two student writes about the agent's output, so year one rehearses the reading step (decision college-0004) a year before there is code to read.
